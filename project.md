# Barry
The voice agent that goes to the doctor with you.
  "You brief Barry once. He walks into every appointment with you."
## Hackathon Theme
  Accessibility tools for navigating the medical care system for disabled users. 
## The Problem

Disabled patients get talked over, asked confusing questions, and have to re-explain their entire story at every appointment. People who struggle to speak or process long, complex questions are often left out of their own care.

## The Solution

The patient tells Barry about themselves once, by voice: conditions, meds, allergies, communication style, and who helps them make decisions. Barry then comes along to appointments, listens to the doctor (with consent), and helps the patient participate.
